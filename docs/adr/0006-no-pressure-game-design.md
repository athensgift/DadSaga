# ADR-0006: No-pressure game design

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Research shows that parenting apps cause guilt and comparison, and that this is a main reason parents stop using them
([Univ. of Edinburgh via Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2018-04-baby-apps-parents-guilty-pressured.html)).
Fathers also report high rates of mental-health problems and find mainstream apps mother-centric
([JMIR Pediatrics 2025](https://pediatrics.jmir.org/2025/1/e64171/PDF)).

## Decision
1. XP rewards the **dad's actions**. Capturing a first gives XP for capturing it, not for the child achieving it.
2. **Nothing is ever lost.** No XP decay, no penalties, no breakable streaks. "This week you shared 3 moments" replaces streaks.
3. Worlds and quests unlock by the child's **age only**. The child's development never gates content.
4. Firsts have **no expected ages** and are never shown as late, missing or compared.
5. A `resilience` skill rewards the dad looking after himself.
6. Health guides are general information with "ask your paediatrician", and no diagnoses.
7. Tone is written for dads: humour, gaming language, no condescension.

## Consequences
- Some "engagement" levers (loss aversion, streak anxiety) are deliberately off-limits.
- Every new feature must be checked against these rules. They are non-negotiable.

# ADR-0019: World map, earlier worlds replayable, and worlds up to age 6

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Only two worlds (0–24 months) existed, so a child over two had nothing to play, and there was no way to see the
journey as a whole.

## Decision
- **Two new worlds** (content only): "The Land of No" (25–41 months: tantrums, potty) and "The Kingdom of Why"
  (42–71 months: nightmares, first day of kindergarten). 10 quests and 2 bosses each, bilingual, with the same
  "general information, ask your paediatrician" approach for boss guides. Children of 6+ still see "coming soon".
- **World map** (sheet from the world header on home), per selected child: every world in order as a path, marked
  current ("you are here"), visited, or locked with "unlocks in N months". Visited and current worlds show progress
  for that child: quests done at least once and bosses beaten.
- **Earlier worlds stay playable.** Tapping a visited world shows its quests and bosses on home, with a "back to the
  current world" button. Choosing another child returns to their current world. Locked worlds cannot be opened:
  content follows the child's age, never a deadline (ADR-0006).
- Age ranges read in months up to two years and in (half) years after, e.g. "9–24 μηνών", "2–3½ ετών".

## Consequences
- Older siblings now have something to play, which makes families (ADR-0012) more useful.
- Content for ages 6+ is the next gap.

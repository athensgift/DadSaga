## Description (4000)

```text
An RPG for new dads and dads-to-be. You're the hero, your kid is your companion, and real life is the campaign.

DadSaga turns fatherhood into a game, from the months before the birth to age six, without turning your child into a scoreboard.

QUESTS
Real things you do with and for your kid: a bath, a bedtime story, a night shift, cuddle time. Tap when you did one. That's all it takes. Most come with a short, practical tip.

BOSS FIGHTS
The hard parts of real life, like The First Fever, The Endless Cry or The Sleep That Vanished, each with a short, calm survival guide.

LEGENDARY MOMENTS
The first smile, the first laugh, the first steps. Keep them with a photo and a note, celebrated when they happen. Never expected by a date, never shown as late.

WORLDS AND TITLES
Five worlds, from the Harbour of Waiting before the birth to the Kingdom of Why. All of them are open; your child's age only says which one you are in. What you do earns you new titles, like "Steady hands", and most bring gear for your hero.

BEFORE THE BIRTH
Add your child as "Not born yet" and your saga starts in the Harbour of Waiting: practical quests for the months before, and the first firsts, like the day you found out. DadSaga never asks for a due date and never counts down.

YOUR SAGA, KEPT AND MADE INTO A FILM
Every quest, boss and first, with your notes and photos, gathered month by month on the Timeline. Turn a year, a world or the whole saga into a short film with music you choose, made on your iPhone.

A GAME WITH NO PRESSURE
- Titles are for what you do. Never for your child's development.
- Nothing is ever lost. No penalties, no streaks to break.
- Every world is open. Only age says which one you are in: never milestones or a due date, and never compared to anyone.
- Look after yourself too. A whole skill, Resilience, is about you.

MADE FOR YOUR FAMILY
- Choose your hero and add up to four children.
- DadSaga asks whether you have a partner and shows only the quests that fit your family.
- A weekly reminder, only if you want one, and a home-screen widget.
- Large text, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion throughout the app.
- In English and Greek.

YOUR FAMILY STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your child's details and photos stay on your iPhone, and in its backups if you make them. Export a copy of your saga as a single file whenever you like, and import it on a new iPhone.

Free, with no in-app purchases.

The guides are general information, not medical advice. For the pregnancy, the birth and your child's health, ask your midwife or doctor.
```

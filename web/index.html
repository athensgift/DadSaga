Before the birth

Your saga starts before the baby does

You don't have to wait for the birth. Add your child, turn on “Not born yet”, and your saga begins in the Harbour of Waiting: “The ship isn't in sight yet. You're getting the pier ready.” DadSaga doesn't ask for a due date and never counts down. The other worlds open after the birth.

A game with no pressure

Parents drop baby apps when they start to feel like guilt. DadSaga is built on four rules it never breaks.

  1. XP is for what you do. Never for your child's development.
  2. Nothing is ever lost. No penalties, no streaks to break.
  3. Worlds open by age. Never by milestones or a due date, and never compared to anyone.
  4. Look after yourself too. A whole skill branch, Resilience, is about you.

Made for tired eyes too: large text, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion work throughout the app.

Your family stays on your phone

No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your child's details and photos stay on your iPhone, and in its backups if you make them.

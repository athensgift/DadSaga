Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: DadSaga does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. What you enter in the app stays on your iPhone unless you export it yourself, and we never see it.

Who we are

DadSaga is made by FAFOUTIS BROTHERS E.E., which is responsible for this policy (the "controller" under the GDPR). You can reach us at hello@dadsaga.app.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your hero's class, skin tone and look (hair, hair colour, beard and glasses), and the gear you picked from your wardrobe.
  • For each child (up to four in the family at a time): name, gender and skin tone, and the birth date once the child is born. A child can be added before the birth, as "not born yet"; then no date is kept, not even a due date.
  • A child you remove in My family stays on your iPhone, hidden, with their name, birth date and everything recorded with them, so you can bring them back, until you choose Delete for good.
  • Whether you have a partner, as you answered it, so the app can show the cards for the two of you.
  • Your progress: the quests, boss fights and moments you logged, with their dates and any notes.
  • The highest title you have reached and the last title card you closed, so a title and its gear stay when you delete an entry.
  • Photos you add to your entries, saved as files inside the app.
  • Your settings, such as the weekly reminder and when its notifications are due, and your last choice of music and dates for the film.

This data is stored only in the app's own storage on your device, protected by iOS, unless you export a copy of your saga (see below). It is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) in the same way as any other app's data, under your Apple account and Apple's terms. The Home Screen widget reads the same data on your device, and can show a child's name and a moment's photo on your Home Screen.

Exporting and importing your data

In Settings › Export and import you can export your saga as one file: a copy you keep or send where you choose. It holds your hero's class, skin tone and look; your children's names, gender, skin tone and birth dates; whether you have a partner; every quest, boss fight and moment you logged, with its date, notes and photos; your wardrobe and titles; and which film offers you answered. It also holds the children you removed in My family, with everything recorded with them. Your appearance and haptics preferences, the weekly reminder and your film's music and dates choices stay on the iPhone and aren't in the file. No film is in it, since films are never kept.

The app makes the file on your iPhone, and you choose where it goes with the iOS share sheet: Files, AirDrop or another app, under that place's terms. Nothing is sent to us. The file has no password, so anyone who has it can open it: keep it where you'd keep your photos. The app deletes its own temporary copy of the file when you leave Export and import (or, if the app closed first, the next time it opens).

Importing, from the same page or with Import data on the first screen after installing, reads only the file you pick. It replaces the saga on that iPhone, everything listed above, with the one in the file; the settings that stay with the iPhone don't change. The app reads the file from a temporary copy of its own, which it deletes as soon as it's done.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for an entry. When you pick photos from your library, iOS gives the app only the photos you picked. The app has no access to the rest of your library.

Saga films

When you ask for a film of your saga, the app makes it on your iPhone from your photos, firsts and notes. Nothing is uploaded. The film is a temporary file: it plays in the app and is deleted when you close it, so the app keeps no copy.

While a film is being made, the app uses Apple's Vision on your iPhone to look at an entry's photos: to choose which of its extra photos play, and to keep faces in view. It guesses whether a face is a child's or an adult's, never who it is. Nothing it finds is stored, shown or sent, and all of it is forgotten when the film is ready.

To keep or send a film, you use the iOS share sheet. What you share goes where you choose, under that app's terms, and it carries the photos and notes it shows. Unless you turn off Dates in the film, it also shows the months it covers and the dates of its firsts and boss fights, which tell how old your child is. If you choose Save Video, iOS asks you once to let DadSaga add to your photo library. That permission only adds: it doesn't let the app see your library.

Notifications

The weekly reminder is a local notification. It is scheduled on your iPhone, goes through no server, and you can turn it off at any time. Like any notification, it can show your child's name and the quest's title on your lock screen.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, share or receive your data or your child's data.

Information from Apple

Apple may share with us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, only if you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone's settings. They contain no names, photos or anything you entered in the app. App Store download numbers also come to us from Apple in aggregate.

Children

DadSaga is made for parents, not for children. The details you enter about your child stay on your device, and we never receive them.

Deleting your data

To erase your saga but keep the app, use Settings › Start over in the app. It removes your hero, children, progress and photos, and keeps only your appearance and haptics preferences and your film's music and dates choices. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Copies in older backups follow your own backup settings. Start over doesn't delete copies of your saga you saved or sent: delete those files wherever you keep them. To delete only a child you removed, use Delete for good in Settings › My family: it deletes that child, with every entry and photo recorded with them, for good. Copies of your saga you saved or sent before keep that child: delete those files wherever you keep them.

If you email us

If you write to hello@dadsaga.app, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and don't share them. The message is delivered through Cloudflare's email routing to our mailbox at Google. A message started from Contact us in the app ends with the app and iOS versions, so we can help; you can delete that line before sending. We keep support emails only as long as we need them to help you, and delete them within 12 months.

This website

dadsaga.app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It uses no analytics and no tracking or advertising cookies. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, and its security features may set a strictly necessary cookie.

Your rights

The only data about you we can hold is an email you sent us. You can ask for a copy of it, or for it to be deleted, at any time. Everything else is on your iPhone and under your control. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Greece, that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example to sync through your own iCloud, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date above.

Contact

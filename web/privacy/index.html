Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: DadSaga does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. What you enter in the app stays on your iPhone, and we never see it.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your hero and your child's profile: names, birth dates and the look you chose.
  • Your progress: the quests, boss fights and moments you logged, with their dates and any notes.
  • Photos you add to moments, saved as files inside the app.
  • Your settings, such as the weekly reminder.

This data is stored only in the app's own storage on your device, protected by iOS. It is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) in the same way as any other app's data, under your Apple account and Apple's terms. The home-screen widget reads the same data on your device.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for a moment. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS gives the app only the photo you picked. The app has no access to the rest of your library.

Notifications

The weekly reminder is a local notification. It is scheduled on your iPhone, goes through no server, and you can turn it off at any time.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, share or receive your data or your child's data.

Information from Apple

Apple may share with us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, only if you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone's settings. They contain no names, photos or anything you entered in the app. App Store download numbers also come to us from Apple in aggregate.

Children

DadSaga is made for parents, not for children. The details you enter about your child stay on your device, and we never receive them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Copies in older backups follow your own backup settings.

If you email us

If you write to hello@dadsaga.app, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and don't share them. The message is delivered through Cloudflare's email routing to our mailbox at Google.

This website

dadsaga.app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example to sync through your own iCloud, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@dadsaga.app

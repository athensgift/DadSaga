Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my saga to a new iPhone?

Transfer your data with an iCloud Backup or Quick Start, as you would for any app. DadSaga doesn't sync between devices yet.

Do I lose anything if I don't open the app for a while?

No. There are no streaks, penalties or deadlines. Your XP, levels and moments are always there when you come back.

Is the boss-fight guide medical advice?

No. The guides are general information to help you stay calm. For anything about your child's health, ask your paediatrician. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How do I change the language?

DadSaga follows your iPhone's language (English or Greek). To choose a language just for DadSaga, open Settings › Language in the app, which takes you to DadSaga's page in your iPhone's Settings.

I don't get the weekly reminder.

Check that it is on in the app, and that notifications are allowed in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › DadSaga. Some quiet weeks are on purpose: there's no reminder in a week when you've already done its quest, and after four weeks without opening the app it stops, so nothing chases you. Open the app and it starts again.

How do I delete everything?

In the app, open Settings › Start over. It erases your hero, children, progress and photos and keeps the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app, unless you export it. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my saga to a new iPhone?

Export it: on your old iPhone, open Settings › Export and import › Make a copy, then Save or send, and send it to the new iPhone with AirDrop or save it in Files. On the new iPhone, install DadSaga and tap Import data on the first screen. DadSaga doesn't sync between devices yet.

How do I export my data, and bring it back?

Open Settings › Export and import › Make a copy, then Save or send. The copy is one file with your family, your hero and every quest, boss fight and moment, with their notes and photos. Keep it somewhere other than your iPhone, such as iCloud Drive in Files or a computer; it has no password, so keep it where you'd keep your photos. To bring it back, open Settings › Export and import and tap Choose a copy under Import data, or tap Import data on the first screen after installing. It replaces the saga on that iPhone with the one in the file.

Can I use DadSaga before my baby is born?

Yes. Add your child and turn on “Not born yet”: your saga starts in the Harbour of Waiting, with quests to get ready for the birth. DadSaga doesn't ask for a due date. When your baby arrives, turn off “Not born yet” in Settings › My family and add the birth date: from then on the worlds open by age.

Do I lose anything if I don't open the app for a while?

No. There are no streaks, penalties or deadlines. Your title, your gear and your moments are always there when you come back, and a title never goes down.

Is the boss-fight guide medical advice?

No. The guides are general information to help you stay calm. For anything about your child's health, ask your paediatrician; for the pregnancy and the birth, your midwife or doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How do I change the language?

DadSaga follows your iPhone's language (English or Greek). To choose a language just for DadSaga, open Settings › Language in the app, which takes you to DadSaga's page in your iPhone's Settings.

I don't get the weekly reminder.

Check that it is on in the app, and that notifications are allowed in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › DadSaga. Some quiet weeks are on purpose: there's no reminder in a week when you've already done its quest, and after four weeks without opening the app it stops, so nothing chases you. Open the app and it starts again.

Where do I find my saga film?

A film isn't stored in the app: it's made on your iPhone when you ask and deleted when you close it. To keep it, tap Share or save while it plays and choose Save Video, or send it to someone. To make one, tap Make a film on the Timeline, or Make the film when DadSaga offers one on Home. If you leave the app while it's being made, it stops; open DadSaga and make it again.

Can I choose the film's music, or leave out the dates?

Yes. Before you make a film, choose one of five original tracks under Music and tap the play button beside it to hear it first. Dates in the film is on by default; turn it off for a film you'll share beyond your family, since dates also tell how old your child is. DadSaga remembers both choices for next time.

How do I delete everything?

In the app, open Settings › Start over. It erases your hero, children, progress and photos and keeps the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device. Start over doesn't touch copies of your saga you saved or sent: they stay wherever you keep them. To delete only a child you removed, use Delete for good in Settings › My family.
